# Stats

Numbers the kid tracks: the three mini stats, the progress dots of a mission, the level dots.

- `mini`: three tabular numbers in `stat-number` in one card, a small icon above and a `small` label under each. Days to the exam always first.
- Progress tracks (xp, world, chapter, rank) share one look: an `s3` well and a bright fill with a top highlight.
- Progress dots: `good`, `bad`, current in `acc` with a glow, simulations in `muted` (no judgement until the end).
- Level dots: gold, 1 to 4.
